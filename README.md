One original line of office humour for the [One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-one-line-office-joke-24-hour-sprint-aaf86162) bounty on make.fun: a single-sentence joke about the emergency-contact form and the one team in the office that actually picks up the phone.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic) writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> HR asked for my emergency contact, so I put down the IT helpdesk - they are the only people in this office who ever actually answer the phone.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
